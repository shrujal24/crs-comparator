What IRCC is changing

One factor is rebuilt; everything else stays

New: Labour Market Integration factor

Replaces the core "Canadian work experience" factor (max 70 with a spouse, 80 without). You get points from either your Canadian work experience or a qualifying job offer, whichever is higher. Never both.

Maximum: 140 points with an accompanying spouse, 150 without.

High-wage occupations

  • 186 occupations (NOC codes) whose median wage is at least 1.3×, 1.5× or 2× the Canada-wide median.
  • Your tier depends on the occupation, not your personal salary.
  • Work experience bonus needs 12+ months (1,560 hours) in listed occupations.

Job offers are back, no LMIA

  • Job offer points were removed on March 25, 2025. Today they are worth 0.
  • From Nov 3: 50, 70 or 100 points for an offer in a high-wage occupation. No LMIA needed.
  • Full-time, non-seasonal, at least one year after you become a PR. Add it to your profile before Nov 3.

Unchanged

  • Age, education, official languages, spouse factors.
  • Skill transferability (it still uses the length of your Canadian experience).
  • Provincial nomination (600), French (25/50), Canadian education (15/30), sibling (15).
  • IRCC will recalculate every profile and pause invitations for up to two weeks.
